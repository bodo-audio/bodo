# Asset attribution

Every non-code asset in this repository (sounds, presets, templates, icons, fonts, images) is listed here with its author, source and license.

**Policy:** only original work by Bodo contributors, or third-party material under a license that allows redistribution (OSI open source, public domain or CC0, or a Creative Commons license that allows redistribution). Nothing is ever taken from a commercial product: no samples, presets, skins, icons or graphics, whether extracted, decompiled or recreated from one ([AGENTS.md section 11](AGENTS.md#11-clean-room-rule)). Each third-party asset is checked individually before it is added, and its license text is kept with it or reproduced below.

Sounds and other content that ship as factory content live in [`assets/factory/`](assets/factory/README.md). Content users make with them belongs to the users.
